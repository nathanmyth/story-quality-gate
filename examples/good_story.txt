Title: Export monthly usage as CSV
As a facility manager, I want to export monthly energy usage as a CSV file, so that I can share consumption trends with the finance team.
Acceptance criteria:
- Given I am on the Usage page, when I select a month and click Export, then a CSV with one row per day downloads
- Given the month has no data, when I click Export, then I see the message "No usage recorded for this month"
Story points: 3
